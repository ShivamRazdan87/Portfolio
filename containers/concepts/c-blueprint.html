How a custom build works

A simple office takes around two weeks from approved drawing to delivery.

  1. Brief

    Tell us what it's for, roughly how big, and where it's going.

  2. Drawing & price

    We send a layout drawing and a fixed quote to approve.

  3. Fabrication

    Built in our yard. You're welcome to visit and check progress.

  4. Delivery

    Craned into place, levelled and handed over ready to use.
