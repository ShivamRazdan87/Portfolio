Section F · FAQ

Good questions

Still unsure? Send us a WhatsApp and we'll answer directly.

Should I buy or rent?

Renting suits short projects, events and temporary site needs. Buying suits long-term use, heavy modification, or keeping it as an asset. We'll help you compare costs for your timeline.

Do you sell used containers?

Yes, along with new units. Used containers are great value for storage and site use. Every unit is inspected before dispatch.

What can be built from a container?

Cafés, shops, kiosks, offices, homes, washrooms, security cabins and more, with doors, windows, insulation, electrics, plumbing and interiors.

What's the difference between 40 ft and 40 ft high cube?

Same length and width, but a high cube is about 30 cm (1 ft) taller. That extra headroom matters for fit-outs, homes and tall cargo.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We arrange transport and place the container at your site. Share your location and access details and delivery will be included in your quote.

How should I prepare my site?

You'll need a firm, level base (compacted ground, blocks or a slab) and clear access for the truck to unload. We'll advise based on your site.

Do you supply refrigerated containers?

Yes. Reefers hold chilled or frozen temperatures for food, pharma and other perishables. They need a suitable power supply on site.
