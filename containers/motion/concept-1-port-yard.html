06 / FAQ

Questions,
answered

Can't find it here? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply directly.

Do you supply new and used containers?

Yes. Used containers are a cost-effective choice for storage and site use. New units suit fit-outs and premium builds. Every unit is inspected before dispatch.

What can you build from a container?

Cafés, shops, kiosks, site offices, homes, washrooms, security cabins and more. We handle cut-outs, doors and windows, insulation, electrics, plumbing, interiors and paint.

Do you deliver to my site?

Yes. We arrange transport and place the container at your site. Share your location and access details and we'll include delivery in your quote.

What do I need to prepare at the site?

A firm, level base (compacted ground, concrete blocks or a slab) and clear access for the delivery truck and unloading. We'll advise based on your site.

What is a high cube container?

A high cube is about 30 cm (1 ft) taller than a standard container, at 9'6" external height. It's ideal for tall cargo and for fit-outs that need extra headroom.
