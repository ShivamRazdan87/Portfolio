FAQ

Common questions

Do you sell new or used containers?

Both. New "one-trip" containers have made a single voyage and look nearly new. Used containers are graded by condition and priced to match, and we send you real photos before you pay.

Can I rent a container instead of buying?

Yes. We offer monthly and long-term rental on shipping, storage and office containers. Delivery and pick-up can be included in the rental.

How long does a custom container take?

Simple modifications such as shelving, a window or an extra door usually take a few days. Full office, shop or home conversions typically take 2–4 weeks depending on the design.

What do I need on site for delivery?

A level, firm surface (concrete pads, blocks or compacted ground) and clear access for a trailer truck. For tight spaces we can arrange a crane. Tell us about your site and we'll advise.

Do you deliver outside the city?

Yes, we deliver across the region. Transport cost depends on distance and is shown separately in your quote.
