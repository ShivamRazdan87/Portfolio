FAQ

Good questions

Where do you deliver?

We deliver within 8 km of our kitchen. Orders above ₹999 ship free; below that it's a flat ₹49.

How do I pay?

Pay by UPI or cash on delivery. We'll share the UPI details on WhatsApp after confirming your order.

Is everything available eggless?

Yes — every item on our menu has an eggless version. Just mention it in your order.

How fresh are the bakes?

Everything is baked to order on the day of delivery. Cakes stay best refrigerated for up to 3 days.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Ready-menu orders can be changed up to 2 hours before delivery. Custom cakes can be changed up to 24 hours before.
