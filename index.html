FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

Do I own the website after it's built?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the code and design are yours.

Do you handle hosting and the domain?

I can set them up for you, but they're billed separately from the build cost (see the Pricing section above).

How long does a project take?

Depends on scope — typical timelines are listed under each pricing tier, from about a week for a simple site to 3–4 weeks for a full corporate build.

Can I request changes after launch?

Minor tweaks are included per your plan. For ongoing changes after that, the monthly maintenance add-on covers it.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your content (text, images, logo if you have one), a sense of what you like design-wise, and the ₹5,000 advance to begin.

Do you build online stores, not just static sites?

Yes — see the Ananya General Store case study above for an example of a full store with payments, login, and an admin dashboard.
