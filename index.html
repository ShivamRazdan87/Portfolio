About

A two-person studio that ships real websites, not just demos.

We’re Shivam and Saksham, a full-stack studio working across the modern web stack (Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Tailwind) to turn ideas for real businesses into websites people actually use.

Recent work includes the portfolio for Stambha Design House, an interior and architecture studio with over 20 years of projects; the website for CraveYardd, a dessert cart in Birmingham; and a complete online store for Ananya General Store in Greater Noida.

Every site is designed from scratch for the business it’s for. Where it helps, we add AI features too: smarter search, conversational support and tools that make everyday work faster.

FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

Do I own the website after it's built?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the code and design are yours.

Do you handle hosting and the domain?

We can set them up for you, but they're billed separately from the build cost (see the Pricing section above).

How long does a project take?

Depends on scope. Typical timelines are shown on each package in the Pricing section, from about a week for a simple site to 3–4 weeks for a full corporate build.

Can I request changes after launch?

Yes. A website care plan covers regular changes every month, or you can pay per request whenever you need something. See the Pricing section for both.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your content (text, images, logo if you have one), a sense of what you like design-wise, and the ₹5,000 advance to begin.

Do you build online stores and admin panels, not just static sites?

Yes. See Ananya General Store in our projects for a full store with payments, logins and an admin dashboard, and Stambha Design House for a site the owner updates from an admin panel.
