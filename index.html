About

A developer who ships to real shopkeepers, not just demos.

I'm a full-stack developer working across the modern web stack — Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Tailwind — to turn ideas for real businesses into working products people actually use.

My most recent build, Ananya General Store, is a complete grocery delivery platform built from scratch for a local shop: product catalog, checkout, delivery slots, an admin dashboard, and a WhatsApp order flow the owner uses every day.

I'm currently extending that same instinct — practical, product-first engineering — into AI-assisted features: smarter search, conversational support, and tools that layer language models on top of everyday workflows.
